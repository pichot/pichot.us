During my [Malmö Residency](/projects/malmo-residency/), I spoke at Coding Cities, an evening at [Media Evolution](https://www.mediaevolution.se/) about technology and the public realm. Martin Thörnkvist introduced it, and I shared the stage with Violet Whitney of Spatial Pixel ([Beyond the Screen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kR9MDEBCgFQ)) and Johan Linåker ([Municipal Collaboration on Open-Source Software](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r70K1JUSlNc)).

My talk was about data-collecting technology in public space, and the mistrust that grows when people can't tell what it is, who runs it, or what it does with their data. I presented Digital Trust for Places and Routines (DTPR), the open source visual language we steward at Helpful Places. I covered how research and taxonomy became signage guidelines, and pilots from Sydney Olympic Park during the Women's World Cup to Angers, France.
