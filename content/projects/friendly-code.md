In the summer of 2012 I founded Friendly Code, the Grand Rapids Code for America Brigade and one of the first brigades in the network. From 2012 to 2015 I was its captain: we held meetups every two weeks, relaunched the [Viget.org](/projects/viget-rebuild/) city wiki, and worked with the City of Grand Rapids to develop and launch its open data portal.
