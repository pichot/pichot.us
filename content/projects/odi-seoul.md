In March 2016 I gave the opening talk at the 9th SAC, a seminar on public linked open data (LOD) and the artificial intelligence industry at D.CAMP in Seoul. Saltlux hosted it, ODI Seoul organized it, and Open Knowledge Korea sponsored it. Representing Five by Five and ODI Paris, I spoke about how public open data is used in Europe and France, with case studies (유럽과 프랑스의 공공(LOD)데이터 활용 전략 및 사례). Machi Takahashi of ODI Osaka and Professor Key-Sun Choi of KAIST also spoke.
