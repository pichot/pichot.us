From 2020 to 2021 I helped build the Incubateur des Territoires, a government digital service incubator within the [Agence nationale de la cohésion des territoires](https://anct.gouv.fr/) (ANCT), the national agency that supports local governments across France. As part of the [beta.gouv.fr](https://beta.gouv.fr/) community, the incubator builds open source software for and with French local governments.
