[The Urban World](https://theurban.world/) is an observatory of urban complexity that tells the story of global urbanization through data. It's inspired by Our World in Data and Gapminder's *Factfulness*, but focused on cities.

I started it during my [Malmö Residency](/projects/malmo-residency/) in 2023, and it launched in September 2026.
